What is Division?

Division is taking a large set and dividing it into smaller, equal-sized pieces.

Special Rules & Properties:

  • Dividing by Zero: Dividing any number by 0 is mathematically impossible / undefined! (e.g., 5 ÷ 0 = Undefined).
  • Dividing by One: Any number divided by 1 remains unchanged. (e.g., 8 ÷ 1 = 8).
