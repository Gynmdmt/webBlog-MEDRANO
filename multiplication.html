What is Multiplication?

Multiplication is multiplying one number by another to find the total of equal groups.

Special Rules & Properties:

  • Zero Property: Any number multiplied by 0 is always equal to 0! (e.g., 100 × 0 = 0).
  • Identity Property: Any number multiplied by 1 stays the exact same. (e.g., 12 × 1 = 12).
