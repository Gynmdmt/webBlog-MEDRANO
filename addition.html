What is Addition?

Addition is taking two or more numbers and putting them together to get a total amount.

Special Properties:

  • Commutative Property: Changing the order of numbers does not change the answer. (e.g., 3 + 5 = 8 and 5 + 3 = 8).
  • Identity Property: Adding 0 to any number leaves it unchanged. (e.g., 9 + 0 = 9).
