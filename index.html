Arithmetic Operations

Basic operations form the foundation of all mathematical calculations. Click any card to read more!

Addition (+)

Combining two or more quantities to calculate a total sum.

Example: 5 + 3 = 8

Subtraction (−)

Taking one quantity away from another to find the difference.

Example: 10 − 4 = 6

Multiplication (×)

Repeated addition of a number by the number of times specified.

Example: 4 × 3 = 12

Division (÷)

Splitting a total quantity into equal parts or groups.

Example: 20 ÷ 5 = 4
