What is Subtraction?

Subtraction is taking a quantity away from another to calculate how much is left.

Special Rules & Properties:

  • Subtracting Larger Numbers: Subtracting a larger number from a smaller number results in a negative number! (e.g., 3 − 5 = -2).
  • Identity Rule: Subtracting 0 from any number gives that same number. (e.g., 7 − 0 = 7).
